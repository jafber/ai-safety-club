Building the AI safety community at HPI.

HAIS is the HPI AI Safety initiative. We are a group of students at the Hasso Plattner Institute dedicated to reducing the risks of future AI systems. Our goal is to help future AI safety researchers and policymakers grow their careers.

Join our Telegram group ↗

The stakes have never been higher.

Over the past years, AI systems have gotten wildly more capable, but this might only be the beginning. Some experts believe that soon, AI could start to recursively improve itself, becoming more capable than the smartest of humans.

If we implement these systems right, they can help us solve humanity's most pressing problems. If tech corporations abuse their powers or lose control over them entirely, the consequences will be catastrophic.
